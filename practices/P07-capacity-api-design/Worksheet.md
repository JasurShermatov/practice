## Step 1 · Write the capacity estimate

- [docs/capacity.md](../../hotel-booking/docs/capacity.md): the course's heading and first line, the
  five assumptions (plus the three sizes the course's program uses: 600 B per row, 2 KB per answer,
  2 MB of photos), the five results, and "To measure later" with the two P08 lines.
- "Work out each number with the calculator first": done as exact fractions in the section
  *Checked by hand* (28,800 ÷ 86,400 = 1/3; × 10 = 10/3 = 200 a minute; × 40 = 400/3 ≈ 133;
  20 × 365 × 600 = 4,380,000 B; 1/6 visitor a second × 2,000 KB ≈ 333 KB/s), then confirmed by the
  program [capacity.py](../../hotel-booking/capacity.py) — the course's own six lines, printed
  identically ([s1-capacity](evidence/logs/s1-capacity.txt)):

  ```text
  Searches per day: 1440 visitors x 20 = 28800
  Searches per second: average 0.33, peak 3.33 (200 per minute)
  Bookings: 20 per day, 0.0023 per second at peak, 1440 searches per booking
  Database queries at peak: 3.33 searches/s x 40 rooms = 133.33 per second
  Reservation storage: 20 x 365 x 600 bytes = 4.38 MB per year
  Bandwidth at peak: search answers 6.67 KB/s, room photos 333.33 KB/s
  ```

- Compared with the first line of `l04-notes.md`: they agree (3.33 a second, 200 a minute) —
  and `tests/test_capacity.py::test_the_peak_agrees_with_the_l04_notes` keeps them agreeing.
- My additions, labelled as mine in the document: a workload model per request type, explicit
  extra assumptions, six more results (write load 0.0069 statements/s, 1,440 reads per write,
  3.33 requests in flight by Little's law, 21.9 MB in five years, 1,333 queries/s at ten times the
  visitors), and "What it means for the design". Every result row in the document is generated by
  `capacity.py` and compared line by line by `tests/test_capacity.py` — a changed assumption
  cannot leave a stale number in the document.
- The assumption "a search asks once per room" was **counted on the real SQL**: 2 statements for
  R7 and R8, 40 for a 40-room catalog (`test_a_search_asks_the_database_once_per_room`).
